# Style notes — Critical Care (Part B3, own words; no copied sentences)

- **Title**: median 18–19 words (IQR 15–23); 58% use a colon (main claim : study type, e.g. "...: a multicentre retrospective study" / "...: development and external validation"). Plain clinical nouns, no model names first.
- **Abstract**: structured — Background / Methods / Results / Conclusions; ~320 words (≤350). Background = 2–3 sentences on the clinical problem and the gap. Methods = data sources, cohort size, outcome definition, model, validation design. Results = cohort numbers first, then key metric with 95% CI, then clinically framed result. Conclusions = 1–2 cautious sentences, often "may" + need for prospective evaluation.
- **Introduction** (titled "Background" or "Introduction"): opens with the clinical burden (incidence, harm), then current practice, then limitation of current tools, then aim in the last paragraph ("We aimed to..."). Short: 3–5 paragraphs. Contributions are written as aims, not bullet lists (we can use one short sentence list of aims).
- **Methods** subsections: Study design and data sources; Population (inclusion/exclusion); Outcome definition; Predictors / data processing; Model development; Comparators; Evaluation; Statistical analysis; Ethics (public de-identified data → waiver statement). TRIPOD(+AI) mentioned in ~20%.
- **Results**: cohort characteristics table first (Table 1), then performance table, then figures (ROC/calibration, clinical utility), subgroup/sensitivity.
- **Discussion**: key findings paragraph → comparison with prior work → clinical implications → strengths and limitations (explicit heading or paragraph) → future work.
- **Conclusion**: short (2–4 sentences).
- **Declarations** at end: Ethics approval and consent to participate; Consent for publication; Availability of data and materials; Competing interests; Funding; Authors' contributions; Acknowledgements. Abbreviations list common.
- **Language**: past tense for methods/results, present tense for interpretation; mostly active or neutral voice; hedged ("was associated with", "may help"). Numbers with 95% CI in brackets. Avoid hype.
- **References**: numbered, Vancouver style, ~37.
- **Figures**: 4–5 multi-panel; clean, clinical labels (minutes, mmHg).
