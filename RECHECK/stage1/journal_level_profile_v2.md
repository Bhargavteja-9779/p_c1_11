# Journal Level Profile v2 — Critical Care (updated 2026-11-23)
Base: first-run profile (36 ML/prediction papers 2024–2026; `Target_Journal_Submission/journal_selection/journal_level_profile.md`) +
10 newest accepted research articles (Oct–Nov 2026, `newest10.md`).
- Expected minimum: clinical problem stated in clinical units; ≥2 independent cohorts (external validation); comparison with what clinicians
  already use (simple bedside parameter or rule); 95% CIs; calibration; honest limitations; Declarations block; data/code availability.
- What lifts a paper above the median: multicentre or multi-setting validation; decision-level results (alarms, missed events, minutes);
  a finding that changes how existing tools are judged (meta-research / evaluation papers are being accepted); reproducible code.
- What is rare and therefore distinctive: seeds/variance reporting, ablation, pre-registered analysis plan, label-free transfer to a new setting.
- Format targets: structured abstract ≤350 words (UNVERIFIED limit), title 15–23 words with colon, body ~4,500 words, 4–5 figures, 2–3 tables,
  30–45 references, Vancouver numbering.
